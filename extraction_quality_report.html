Interpretation

What the comparison says

MarkItDown is the pragmatic default.

Its Markdown is inexpensive, preserves all 45 address components in this set, and gives the highest count of fully correct contracts when paired with the LLM.

NuExtract Markdown is the best source overall.

Across both extractors it scores 94.0%. Paired with the LLM it ties the top field score; paired with NuExtract it remains close behind.

Do not use the general LLM directly on these PDF pages.

It scores 58.7%, with 17 missing and 14 wrong fields. Contract 15 scores 0/5.

Direct NuExtract is a credible simpler pipeline.

At 92.0%, it trails the best two-stage route by only 2.7%, without generating intermediate Markdown.

Explicit-total logic needs reinforcement.

Contracts 03 and 10 state components but no total; most routes incorrectly return base rent or calculate a sum. Contract 15 states €1,205 explicitly, yet six routes return null.

Cross-page name merging is the other weak point.

Contract 15 is missed by every route, and contracts 01, 09, and 11 expose accent, page-merging, and multiple-renter failures.
